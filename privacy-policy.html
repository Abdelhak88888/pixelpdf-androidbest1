PixelPDF Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Overview

PixelPDF is a document utility designed to help you convert, edit, compress, and extract text from files. We respect your privacy and follow a local-first approach wherever the selected feature allows it. This policy explains what the app does, what information may be processed by integrated services, and the choices available to you.

2. Files and images

Images and PDF files that you select are processed by the app on your device for the requested operation. PixelPDF does not create a user account and does not intentionally upload your selected files to a PixelPDF server for conversion, editing, compression, or local OCR. Files remain under your control unless you explicitly choose an external sharing, translation, advertising, or system action.

3. OCR and translation

OCR recognition is performed in the app using the selected language models. If you enable OCR translation, the extracted text is sent to the external translation service used by the app so that a translated result can be returned. Do not translate confidential or sensitive text unless you accept that this limited text-processing request is handled by that external service. Translation is optional and is not required for ordinary local conversion or editing.

4. Downloads and local storage

When you download a PDF, image, TXT file, or OCR result, Android saves it in the Downloads/PixelPDF folder through the system MediaStore. The app may store local preferences such as language choice, credit balance, ad-count limits, and PRO state on the device. These local values are not a cloud account and are not used to identify you personally.

5. Huawei services, advertising, and purchases

PixelPDF integrates Huawei Mobile Services, including Huawei Ads and Huawei In-App Purchases. Huawei services may process technical information required to load ads, measure ad delivery, prevent fraud, manage consent choices, or complete a purchase. Rewarded ads are optional; credits are added only after Huawei confirms the rewarded callback. Purchases are processed by Huawei IAP, and PixelPDF does not receive or store your payment card credentials. Please review Huawei’s own privacy information for processing performed by Huawei services.

6. Permissions and device access

The app requests access only when needed to select user files, display the WebView interface, connect to required online services, and save files through Android storage APIs. PixelPDF does not request location, contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone, or camera access for advertising or analytics. The app does not secretly monitor the device, modify other apps, or collect permanent device identifiers for unrelated purposes.

7. Security and retention

PixelPDF does not maintain a PixelPDF cloud repository of your documents. Temporary data created during processing is held in app memory or temporary device storage as needed for the operation and should be removed by the app or the operating system after use. You should still avoid selecting documents that you do not have permission to process and should protect files saved in your device’s Downloads folder.

8. Your choices and rights

You may use the local document tools without creating an account. You may decline optional advertising personalization where the device or Huawei settings provide that choice, decline optional translation, delete downloaded files from your device, clear the app’s local storage, or uninstall the app. For privacy questions, requests, or concerns about third-party processing, contact us using the address below and identify the relevant request without sending confidential documents.

9. Children

PixelPDF is a general-purpose document utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit a child’s personal or sensitive information to any optional translation or external service through the app.

10. Policy updates

We may update this policy when the app’s features, integrated services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will show the latest update date. Continued use after an update means that you have had an opportunity to review the revised policy.

11. Contact and privacy requests

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or other privacy concerns, contact nosayr.eddin@gmail.com. Please do not send confidential documents or payment information by email.
